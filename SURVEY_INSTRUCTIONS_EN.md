# MOOVED E-Bike Market Survey - Instructions for Sales Team

## 📋 Overview

We are conducting a market survey to gather data on electric bicycles (e-bikes) available in Ghana. This information will help us understand the local market, pricing, and product specifications.

**Your participation is important!** Every submission helps us make better business decisions.

---

## 🔗 How to Access the Survey

1. Open your mobile phone browser (Chrome, Safari, or any browser)
2. Go to: **http://localhost:5000**
   - *Note: You must be connected to the same WiFi network as the server*
3. If you can't access via localhost, ask your manager for the server's IP address and use: **http://[server-ip]:5000**

---

## 📝 Step-by-Step Guide

### Step 1: Fill in Your Basic Information
- **Salesperson Name**: Enter your full name
- **Region**: Select your region from the dropdown list (e.g., Greater Accra, Ashanti, Western, etc.)
- **Specific District/City**: Enter the city or district where you're collecting data (e.g., Tamale, Kumasi, Takoradi)

### Step 2: Add Vehicle Models (One Submission = Multiple Models)
You can submit **multiple e-bike models** in one form! Here's how:

#### For Each Vehicle Model:
1. **Vehicle Photo**: Click the upload area to take a photo or select an image from your phone
   - Alternatively, paste a photo URL if you have one
   
2. **Range (km)**: Enter the battery range in kilometers (e.g., 60 km)
   
3. **Charging Time (hours)**: Enter how long it takes to fully charge (e.g., 6 hours)
   
4. **Weight (kg)**: Enter the total weight of the bike in kilograms (e.g., 35 kg)
   
5. **Price (GHS)**: Enter the selling price in Ghana Cedis (e.g., 4200)
   
6. **Notes**: Optional - enter brand name, model name, dealer info, or any other details

7. **Add Another Model**: Click the "+ Add Another Model" button to add more vehicle models to the same submission

### Step 3: Submit Your Survey
- Review all information
- Click **"Submit Survey"** button
- You'll see a success message when submitted
